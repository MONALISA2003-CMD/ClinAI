# Deep Functional Modules

## Care
Registration, patient identity, duplicate detection, appointments, smart scheduling, queues, check-in, triage, encounters, SOAP/progress/procedure/discharge notes, diagnoses, observations, orders, care plans, referrals, follow-up and care gaps.

## Clinical services
Lab accessioning, specimen collection/barcodes, processing, analyzer interfaces, result verification/critical values; pharmacy verification, interactions, dispensing, batches and expiry; imaging orders, DICOM/PACS boundary and reports; procedure consent and documentation.

## Hospital
Emergency acuity, resuscitation workflow, admissions, wards, bed board, transfers, nursing tasks, medication administration record, rounds and discharge reconciliation; surgery, maternity, pediatrics and immunization workflows.

## Finance/supply
Charge capture, invoices, payments, mobile money/card adapters, insurance eligibility/preauth/claims, suppliers, purchase orders, receiving, inventory batches, expiry, stock movements and reorder signals.

## Patient
Portal/mobile, appointment management, results, prescriptions, payments, documents, secure messaging, reminders, telemedicine boundary and patient-generated measurements.

## Intelligence
Role-specific Command Center, operational KPIs, patient-flow analytics, population health, care-gap detection, governed AI assistance, RAG, model/version audit and evaluation.

## Trust
Tenant isolation, RBAC/permissions, MFA, consent, audit, encryption, secrets, backups, monitoring, data lifecycle and privacy controls.

## Interoperability
FHIR mapping/API, HL7v2 adapters, DICOM/DICOMweb boundary, DHIS2 adapter, external labs, payment providers and messaging providers.
