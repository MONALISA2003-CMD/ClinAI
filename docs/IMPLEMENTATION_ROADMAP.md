# ClinAI Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 0 Foundation
Identity, organizations, facilities, RBAC, audit, PostgreSQL, migrations, API contracts, CI/CD, observability.

## Phase 1 Core Care
Patient 360, registration, appointments, queue, triage, encounters, notes, diagnoses, observations, orders, care plans, referrals.

## Phase 2 Clinical Services
Laboratory, sample lifecycle, results verification, pharmacy, dispensing, medication safety, inventory, procurement, imaging and procedures.

## Phase 3 Finance
Billing, payments, insurance eligibility, preauthorization, claims, refunds, reconciliation.

## Phase 4 AI
AI gateway, clinical context, RAG, Gemini adapter, Python ML service, safety, human approval, AI audit/evaluation.

## Phase 5 Interoperability
FHIR resources/API, HL7v2 adapters, DICOM/DICOMweb, DHIS2 adapters, external labs and payment providers.

## Phase 6 Patient Ecosystem
Patient portal, mobile, notifications, WhatsApp/SMS/email/push, secure messaging, telemedicine.

## Phase 7 Hospital
Emergency, inpatient, beds, nursing, MAR, rounds, discharge, surgery, maternity, pediatrics.

## Phase 8 Intelligence
BigQuery analytics, population health, care gaps, operational intelligence, forecasting and governed research datasets.
