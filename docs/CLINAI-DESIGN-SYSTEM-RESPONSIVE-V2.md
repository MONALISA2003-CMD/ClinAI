# ClinAI Responsive Design System V2

This release establishes the ClinAI visual identity and responsive experience before V14.

## Brand

- The supplied ClinAI logo is the official product mark.
- The supplied ClinAI symbol is used for compact mobile and tablet branding.
- ClinAI Blue is the main brand colour.
- ClinAI Red is reserved for important and critical moments.
- White and calm clinical neutrals provide the main surfaces.

## Experience principle

ClinAI does not shrink. ClinAI reorganizes.

### Phone

The phone experience is task first and touch first. It prioritizes the next useful action, keeps navigation at the bottom, and moves detailed patient information into focused screens.

### Tablet

The tablet experience behaves as a compact clinical desk. A narrow navigation rail keeps the main work area open while the content uses the available width intelligently.

### Laptop

The laptop experience remains a full clinical workstation with persistent navigation, richer dashboards, multi column content and denser records.

## Navigation

Desktop uses the full navigation menu.

Tablet uses a compact navigation rail and menu drawer.

Mobile uses Home, Patients, Queue, Tasks and More.

## Mobile hierarchy

The home screen prioritizes:

1. Today
2. Search
3. The most important numbers
4. Quick actions
5. Patient journey

The complete patient journey is available through the patient care flow rather than taking over the home screen.

## Language

The visible experience uses plain, human language. Developer notes, implementation notes, infrastructure wording and internal system descriptions are not presented as part of the product experience.
